# Fire FC — Launch Ad Creative (FB/IG + TikTok)

**Goal:** 50 paying founding clubs this month · ~$10/team/mo, 3-mo minimum, no trial · **FIFA card = hero visual.**
Positioning: Fire FC *runs your team's whole week and keeps kids touching the ball between practices* —
coach saves hours, parents never miss a game, kids chase a FIFA card. (Family ops + motivation engine,
not a "development platform.") Copy is emoji-free for clean handoff; add native emoji sparingly in body text.

## D) Targeting, placement & spend

- **Geos:** mid/small metros, dense youth soccer, cheap CPMs — Rockford, Peoria, Madison, Quad Cities, Fort Wayne, Des Moines, Green Bay, Springfield, Grand Rapids, Kalamazoo. Exclude top-15 DMAs at first.
- **Meta — buyers:** interests TeamSnap, SportsEngine, GameChanger, US Youth Soccer, AYSO, US Club Soccer, "soccer coaching"; age 28-55; also a broad Advantage+ set (the card creative finds soccer people fast).
- **Meta — parents (pressure/shares):** youth soccer, soccer mom, EA Sports FC/FIFA (unlocks Concept 1), training apps. Optimize for engagement, not conversion.
- **Lookalikes:** seed 1-3% from 75% video viewers + engagers after week 1. Retarget all engagers + site visitors with Concept 4.
- **TikTok:** post TT1-TT3 organic 3-5x/wk from a founder-face account; Spark-Ads the winner (TT2 most likely to break out).
- **First dollars:** 50% Concept 2 (buyer pain = best cold conversion), 35% Concept 1 (scroll-stopper builds the retarget pool + parent pressure), hold Concept 4 for retargeting from day 3, Concept 3 for week 2 once demo calls exist. 15% split.
- **Funnel:** no-trial paid → don't send cold traffic to checkout. One-page site: card visual → 60s demo → founding-50 counter → Stripe checkout (teams/coaches) OR "Book a 15-min club call" (directors). Track the counter honestly.

**Designer/editor checklist:** (1) FIFA card in 2-3 club color schemes + a gold founding-badge asset, (2) split-screen group-chat-vs-digest static, (3) white-labeled "Eastside Strikers" UI mock, (4) founding-counter template w/ editable number, (5) TT2 real-kid shoot w/ signed releases — schedule first, it gates the best TikTok.
