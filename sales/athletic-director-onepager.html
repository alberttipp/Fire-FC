One app for your whole athletic program

Scheduling, parent communication, live game scoring, and real player development — in a single app that families actually open every day.

Right now most programs run on a patchwork of group texts, spreadsheets, and paper — and nothing that actually develops the athlete. Parents miss updates, coaches chase RSVPs, and there's no record of a kid's growth. This replaces all of it with one tool built for youth sports, by a coach.

What it does

📅

Schedule & RSVP

One-tap availability per player. Coaches see a live headcount for every practice and game.

💬

Team chat + push

Announcements and reminders that reach every parent's phone — no more "I didn't see the text."

⚽

Live game scoring

A sideline parent taps the score; goals & assists are tracked and the whole team follows live.

🃏

Player development cards

FIFA-style player ratings coaches update over the season — kids and parents see growth over time.

🎯

At-home training

Drills, challenges, and streaks that get kids practicing on their own — and log it automatically.

📊

Director dashboard

Attendance and engagement across every team at a glance, plus a nightly summary in your inbox.
