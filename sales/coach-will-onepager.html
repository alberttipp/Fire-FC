What it does for your team

📘

The Playbook — teaches each kid their job in YOUR 4-3-1

Paste your coaching videos and the app watches them and writes a simple lesson for every position — what to do when we have the ball, when we don't, and the moment it changes. Each kid taps their spot, learns their role, passes a quick quiz, and earns a badge. Your system, in their pocket.

Fully customizable · built from your video
🃏

FIFA-style player cards

Every kid gets a slick player card they want to level up — their photo, position, ratings and badges. Development that finally feels like a game to a 10-year-old.

📋

Evaluations & simple IDPs

Rate players on a clean FIFA-style card and each kid gets a simplified Individual Development Plan — clear strengths and 2–3 things to work on. No spreadsheets.

🏆

Team leaderboard — home + practice

Tracks every player's touches and training minutes — at practice and the work they put in at home. The whole team, ranked by real effort. Kids compete to climb it.

🔥

Fun competitions

Juggling and first-touch challenges that keep kids training all week — baselines, streaks, and a live leaderboard. Engagement is the point, not an afterthought.

🤖

Automated, so you can coach

Weekly solo challenges auto-assign to the team, your schedule syncs in, families get their own reminders, and you get a simple weekly report on who's engaged. The app handles the admin; you handle the soccer.
